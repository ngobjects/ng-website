1 · Directions 2 · Typefaces 3 · Roots 4 · Errors 5 · wonder-slim 6 · Lemongrass

wonder-slim has no logo. Its README says it improves Project Wonder "not by adding stuff but by removing stuff", so this first round is about the word: slim, cut back, carved, folded, tightened. Each at 180, 64, 32 and 16 pixels, and on dark with the wordmark. The verdict: nice, but generic, without the character the ginger gives engifer. That led to lemongrass.
