What does erroneous ginger look like? Four ways a root can be unwell, each matched to a kind of problem, then inline messages built on them, to replace the dog face that ng shows today. A concept only: nothing here is in the framework.

Ginger in trouble

At 128, 32 and 20 pixels. The inline messages use them at about 30.

Healthy

For reference: variant 5, all of it.

Snapped

An error. Broken at the middle joint, the halves sag apart, fresh pale flesh and a few fibres at the break.

Curled

Unknown key. The shoot curls into a question mark: it grew, but not where you asked.

Wilted

A warning. The root is fine; the shoot has gone limp and olive. Worth a look, not broken.

Shrivelled

Expired. A session or page that sat too long: dried, wrinkled, the leaf curled brown.
